# Brand Management Skill

A brand management skill (ckm:brand) designed to maintain consistency across voice, visual identity, messaging, and assets.

## Core Capabilities

- **Voice & Messaging**: Define tone of voice and create messaging frameworks
- **Visual Standards**: Manage color palettes, typography, and style guides
- **Asset Management**: Organize, validate, and approve brand materials
- **Consistency**: Audit and enforce brand compliance across touchpoints

## Workflow

`docs/brand-guidelines.md` serves as the source of truth, then propagates changes to design tokens and CSS variables. This ensures consistency across different implementation contexts.

## Practical Tools

- Extracting brand context for prompt injection
- Validating asset naming and specifications
- Comparing colors against established palettes
- Syncing guidelines across design systems

## Structure

Documentation is organized by topic (voice frameworks, logo usage, typography, etc.) with accompanying templates and checklists to guide implementation.
