# Design Skill

This is a comprehensive design skill that unifies brand identity, UI styling, logo generation, corporate identity programs, presentations, banners, icons, and social media content creation.

## Key Capabilities

The skill supports **55+ logo styles**, **50+ CIP deliverables**, and **22 banner art directions** across platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube.

## Main Features

**Logo Design** leverages AI to generate logos in 55+ styles with 30 color palettes. Users can search by style, industry, or color preference, then generate designs with white backgrounds.

**CIP (Corporate Identity Program)** produces 50+ branded deliverables across 20 styles and industries, including business cards, letterheads, and office materials with optional logo integration.

**Presentations** uses HTML with Chart.js for strategic pitch decks, supported by copywriting formulas and layout patterns.

**Banner Design** covers social media, advertisements, web, and print formats at correct dimensions (Instagram Stories at 1080×1920px, YouTube headers at 2560×1440px, etc.).

**Social Photos** creates multi-platform images via HTML/CSS screenshots with exact pixel dimensions for each platform.

**Icon Design** generates 15 SVG styles in outlined, filled, duotone, and other formats.

## Workflow Integration

The skill routes tasks to sub-skills (brand, design-system, ui-styling) for generation, search, and rendering functions.
