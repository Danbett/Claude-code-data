# Banner Design Skill

A multi-format creative banner system for generating social media covers, ad banners, website heroes, and print assets using AI-powered visuals.

## Key Capabilities

Designs banners across **platforms** (Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Google Display) and **styles** (minimalist, gradient, bold typography, geometric, neon, glassmorphism, 3D, and more).

## Core Workflow

1. **Gather Requirements** — Clarify purpose, platform, content, brand guidelines, style preference, and quantity
2. **Research & Art Direction** — Use the `ui-ux-pro-max` skill to establish 2-3 complementary design directions
3. **Design & Generate** — Create HTML/CSS banners with exact platform dimensions
4. **Export to Images** — Screenshot banners at exact dimensions with kebab-case naming conventions
5. **Present & Iterate** — Display all options side-by-side with rationale, then refine based on feedback

## Design Standards

- **Safe zones**: Central 70-80% for critical content
- **Max 2 typefaces** per banner
- **Single CTA** per banner
- **4.5:1 contrast ratio** minimum for accessibility

## Platform Dimensions

- Instagram Stories: 1080×1920px
- YouTube headers: 2560×1440px
- Facebook covers: 820×312px
- LinkedIn banners: 1584×396px

**Out of scope:** Video editing, full website design, and print production.
